The four ways to write the same colour

HEX is what designers copy from a mockup — six hexadecimal digits, two per channel, in RGB order. RGB says the same thing in decimal. HSL and HSV are attempts to describe a colour the way people perceive it: a hue angle, plus how saturated and how bright it is.

That last distinction is why HSL is better for building a palette. To make a button darker in HSL you lower L and every shade stays recognisably the same colour. In RGB you have to guess at three channel values and the result usually shifts hue along the way.

HSL and HSV are not the same

They share a hue axis but differ in the other two: HSL's lightness runs from black through the pure colour to white, while HSV's value runs from black to the pure colour only. A colour at HSL 50% lightness and HSV 50% value look nothing alike. CSS uses HSL; most colour pickers and computer-vision tools use HSV. Mixing them up produces palettes that look subtly wrong without an obvious reason why.

Contrast and accessibility

WCAG measures contrast as a ratio between the relative luminance of two colours, from 1:1 (identical) to 21:1 (black on white). The thresholds for normal text:

  • 4.5:1 — AA, the minimum you should ship.
  • 7:1 — AAA, for body text in high-stakes contexts.
  • 3:1 — the relaxed bar for large text (18pt+, or 14pt bold).

A colour that looks fine to you can still fail at 2.8:1. It is one of the cheapest accessibility fixes available, and the check is arithmetic — the numbers above the preview are computed, not estimated.

Frequently asked questions

Why does #FFF sometimes mean the same as #FFFFFF?

The three-digit shorthand works when each channel has two identical digits: #ffffff → #fff, #ff8800 → #f80, but #0d9488 cannot be shortened. This tool always emits the full six-digit form because it is unambiguous.

What about alpha transparency?

Eight-digit hex (#RRGGBBAA) and rgba() both carry alpha. This converter ignores it — the maths for HSL conversion produces a colour, not a colour-plus-opacity, and blending depends on what is behind it.

Is the conversion lossless?

Round-tripping HEX → HSL → HEX can shift by one unit per channel, because HSL stores hue as a float angle and RGB channels are integers. The error is invisible, but if you are diffing colour values in a test, compare with a tolerance rather than for exact equality.
