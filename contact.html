Contact

Bug reports, tool requests and corrections are all welcome. One person reads this inbox, so replies may take a couple of days.

Email

hello@toolbench-tools.pages.dev

What gets a fast reply

  • Wrong output. Include the exact input, what you expected, and what the tool produced. If the input is a piece of JSON or Base64, paste it — there is no privacy concern, because it never reached a server.
  • A broken page. Browser name and version, plus what you clicked. Screenshots help.
  • A tool request. Say what job you were trying to do, not just the tool name. "Convert a SQL INSERT dump into a CSV" is more useful than "SQL tool".
  • An inaccuracy. If something explained on a tool page is technically wrong, I want to fix it.

What you will not get a reply about

  • Link insertion requests, guest post offers and paid "collaboration" pitches. These are deleted unread.
  • Requests to remove content that is not on this site.
  • Anything asking for the site's traffic numbers or advertising rates beyond what is publicly listed.

Privacy note

Email sent to the address above passes through a third-party mail provider and is retained only as long as needed to resolve the issue. Do not send credentials, private keys or customer data — no support request requires them. See the privacy policy for how the site itself handles data.
