Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026. This policy explains what data this site does and does not handle.

The short version

Everything you type into these tools is processed by JavaScript running in your own browser tab. It is never transmitted to a server, never stored, and never seen by anyone but you. The only data collection on this site comes from advertising cookies, described below.

What we do not collect

  • We do not transmit, log or store the content you paste into any tool.
  • There is no account system, so there is no name, email address or password to collect.
  • There are no marketing emails or newsletters.
  • We do not sell, rent or share personal data with data brokers.

Local storage

This site stores exactly one item in your browser's localStorage: a key named tb-theme holding either "light" or "dark". It exists so the site remembers your colour preference between visits. It contains no identifier and is never transmitted anywhere. Clearing your browser data removes it.

Advertising and cookies

This site is supported by advertising served through Google AdSense. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites.

  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the internet.
  • You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • You can also opt out of third-party vendor cookies for personalised advertising at aboutads.info or Your Online Choices (EU visitors).
  • For more information about how Google uses data from sites that partner with it, see Google's partner sites policy.

Advertising cookies are set by Google and its partners, not by this site. We have no access to their contents. If you are in a region covered by the GDPR, UK GDPR or similar legislation, you will be asked for consent before personalised advertising cookies are set, and you may withdraw that consent at any time through the consent banner.

Server logs

This site is served as static files by a hosting provider. Like all web hosts, that provider may record standard request logs — IP address, timestamp, requested URL, user agent — for security and abuse prevention. We do not use these logs to build profiles and do not combine them with any other data.

Analytics

This site does not currently run a third-party analytics product. If that changes, this section will be updated before any tracking is enabled, and the change will be reflected in the "last updated" date above.

Children

This site is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them. Since no personal information is collected from any visitor, no special provisions are required, but we ask that parents supervise children's use of online tools generally.

Your rights

Because this site does not collect personal data, there is nothing for us to export or delete on request. For data held by Google's advertising systems, use the opt-out links above, or your Google account's activity controls. If you believe this site holds data about you and want it removed, contact us and we will investigate.

External links

Pages on this site may link to third-party resources. Those sites operate under their own privacy policies, which we do not control and cannot speak for.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, the "last updated" date at the top will change and, where appropriate, a notice will appear on the homepage. Continued use of the site after a change constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the address on the contact page.
